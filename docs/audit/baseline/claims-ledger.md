# Baseline: Claims Ledger (Pass 1)

Every claim in `/CLAUDE.md` and `/docs` that asserts an enforcement mechanism or a verifiable state of the system, with how the audit checked it and the result. Guidance that asserts nothing checkable ("prefer the simplest implementation") is not a claim and is not here.

Result values: **Proven** (the mechanism exists and was observed or executed), **Partial** (exists but narrower than claimed, or proven only at one level), **Prose** (no mechanism; the rule is documentation or process only), **Contradicted** (the repository shows the opposite), **Unverified** (depends on an external system; see `external-state.md`).

Method values are the evidence grades: RUN, READ, CONSOLE, INFER, UNVERIFIED.
