## Product Opportunity

Signal One Sound has the potential to become much more than an event directory. If executed well, it could become the largest digital hub for revival movements, churches, ministries, evangelists, prayer initiatives, and Christian events in North America. The opportunity is significant because there isn't a widely adopted platform that aggregates these events across denominations and independent ministries. Ironically, many churches are still relying on Facebook posts that disappear into the algorithmic void after about six hours.

## Community Optional Features

**Source status:** Possible Phase 1 or Phase 2 features; paid/free placement is undecided.

### Individual User Concepts

- Submit 1–5 star reviews per event
- Share testimonies per event (300 ct; add filter to block negative requests and comments)
- Post prayer requests (300 ct; add filter to block negative requests and comments)
- Allow prayer response with praying hands and/or 300-word-count reply
- Upload revival photos: 3 max per user per event; add filter to block unsafe photos
- Upload short video reels: 30 sec max; add filter to block unsafe video

### Overall Mobilization Concepts

- **The “Fire-Map” — Interactive Activation:** Live interactive map where users can pin local community needs (e.g., food distribution, street ministry, family needing prayer). Believers nearby receive a push notification to join the deployment.
- **Spontaneous Prayer Walks:** Map a user's walking route via GPS for meetups or to highlight local neighborhoods and strategic locations with prayer prompts.
- **Evangelism Teams:** Geolocation tool that lets individuals join or form “strike teams” for local outreach.
- **Live-Stream “Fire Gatherings”:** Allow leaders or users to broadcast live, or alert users when a church/ministry goes live, for prayer, worship, or open-air preaching.
- **The “Encounters” Testimony Feed:** Rapid-fire visual feed where users upload 30–60 second testimonies **[UNDECIDED: maybe only stays up 30 days]**.
- **Instant Action Alerts:** High-priority notifications to users within a 5–10 mile radius when urgent physical/spiritual need arises.
- **Digital Gospel Track Tool:** Simple visual tool to help users walk someone through the Gospel in multiple languages during a street/divine appointment.
- **Individual Impact Journal Dashboard:** Personal dashboard journaling the user's kingdom footprint (hours served, events attended, community service, etc.) to encourage consistent active faith.
- **Local Action Marketplace:** Registry connecting individuals with vetted local Christian nonprofits, ministries, or faith-based recovery hubs needing volunteers.

# Long-Term Vision

The app could eventually become a broader Signal One Sound ecosystem. Integrated products might include:

- **Revival Finder** — discovering events
- **Prayer Network** — 24/7 prayer coverage
- **Kingdom Marketplace** — connecting churches with speakers, musicians, and ministry vendors
- **Revival Media** — livestreams, podcasts, and teaching
- **Kingdom Resources** — curriculum, books, and ministry tools (ED Marketplace)
- **Mission Connect** — linking volunteers, missionaries, and service opportunities

The strategy is to begin by solving one specific problem extremely well, then expand into adjacent services that reinforce each other. If the platform becomes the first place churches think of when planning an event and the first place believers open when looking for one, the network effects become its greatest asset. That's considerably harder than writing code, but also far more valuable.
